# Astra Literature Stage

Use `astra_search_papers` with several precise queries. Make at most four focused searches, then submit the required output; do not browse job logs or session transcripts. Prefer primary sources and stable OpenAlex, DOI, or arXiv identifiers. Separate retrieved facts from interpretation, compare the closest work directly, and submit at least three source refs. Never invent citations.
